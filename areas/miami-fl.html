For families all across Miami

From Brickell to Little Havana to Westchester, Miami families find a bilingual, family-centered team in Doral.

Book a free 15-minute consultation(305) 594-5658

Miami is wonderfully diverse, and so are the families we serve. Our clinicians understand the realities of city life, long commutes and multicultural homes, and we tailor care to fit.

Telehealth makes it easy for busy downtown professionals to fit therapy into a lunch break, while families with young children enjoy our play-therapy room in Doral.

Getting here

From downtown Miami, take SR-836 west to the Palmetto Expressway north and exit at NW 36th Street, about 20 minutes outside rush hour.
