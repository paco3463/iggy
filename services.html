Our services

Thoughtful, bilingual care for every stage of family life, from a toddler’s tantrums to a couple’s fresh start.

Therapy

Weekly or every-other-week sessions focused on real change you can feel at home, at school and at work.

  • Child therapyPlay-based therapy for big feelings, behavior, anxiety and school struggles.
  • Teen therapyA real, judgment-free space for anxiety, mood, self-esteem and school pressure.
  • Individual therapy for adultsSupport for anxiety, depression, stress, parenting pressure and life transitions.
  • Family therapyBetter communication and less conflict for families navigating change.
  • Couples therapyRebuild connection, trust and teamwork, including premarital counseling.

Assessment and family support

Clear answers, practical coaching and specialized support when a family needs more than talk therapy.

  • Psychological evaluationsADHD, autism, learning and gifted testing with reports you can actually use.
  • Parent coaching and PCITLive coaching for tantrums, defiance and challenging behavior in young kids.
  • Trauma therapy and EMDREMDR and trauma-focused CBT for children, teens and adults.
