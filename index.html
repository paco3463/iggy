    Care that feels like coming home

    Healthy Solutions for Children and Families is a bilingual psychology practice in Doral. We started with a simple belief: when one person in a family is struggling, everyone feels it, and everyone can be part of getting better.

    So we built a place that feels less like a clinic and more like a living room. Soft light, toys on low shelves, a couch you can sink into. And clinicians who speak your language, understand your culture, and know Miami.

    • English and SpanishEvery clinician sees clients in both languages.
    • Children through adultsFrom preschoolers to grandparents, and everyone in between.
    • Therapy and evaluationsOngoing support plus ADHD, autism and learning testing under one roof.
    • Doral or your living roomVisit our office or meet by secure video anywhere in Florida.

    Questions families ask

    Do you take insurance?

    We work with many major insurance plans and also offer self-pay rates. During your free consultation we’ll verify your benefits so there are no surprises.

    Do you offer sessions in Spanish?

    Yes. Every clinician on our team provides therapy and evaluations in both English and Spanish, and many families mix both in the same session.

    How young can a child start therapy?

    We see children as young as 2 for parent-child interaction therapy and 3 and up for play-based therapy. Parents are always part of the process.

    Is telehealth as effective as in-person therapy?

    For most concerns, yes. Research shows video therapy works well for anxiety, depression and parent coaching. Younger children and evaluations usually do best in person.

    How soon can we be seen?

    Most families have a first appointment within one to two weeks of their consultation call.
