Our story

Healthy Solutions for Children and Families began in Doral with a handful of clinicians and a clear goal: give Miami families the kind of thoughtful, bilingual care that’s often hard to find.

Many of the families we meet are juggling two languages, two cultures and a full schedule. Some are new to therapy and unsure what to expect. Others have tried before and didn’t feel heard. We designed every part of our practice, from the first phone call to the colors on our walls, to make that first step feel lighter.

Today our team includes licensed psychologists, mental health counselors, a marriage and family therapist and a clinical social worker, all working together under one roof.

What guides us

  • Family first

    Children don’t live in isolation. We bring parents, siblings and caregivers into the work whenever it helps.

  • Culture matters

    We understand bicultural homes, immigration journeys and the beautiful complexity of Miami families.

  • Evidence with heart

    We use approaches backed by research, delivered with warmth, patience and humor.

  • Real collaboration

    With your permission, we coordinate with schools, pediatricians and psychiatrists so everyone pulls in the same direction.

Our approach

Your first session is about listening. We want to understand your family’s strengths, your history and what you hope will be different. From there we build a plan together, with clear goals you can see progress on.

We draw on cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT), play therapy, parent-child interaction therapy (PCIT), dialectical behavior therapy (DBT) skills, EMDR and family systems work, choosing what fits rather than forcing one method on everyone.

A space designed to feel calm

Our Doral office was designed with families in mind: a play-therapy room stocked for every age, quiet rooms for teens and adults, and a waiting area that feels more like a living room than a lobby.

Insurance and fees

We accept many major insurance plans and offer transparent self-pay rates. We’ll verify your benefits before your first visit and provide superbills for out-of-network reimbursement when needed.

Meet the people behind the practice

Every clinician here is licensed in Florida, fluent in English and Spanish, and chosen as much for their warmth as their expertise.
